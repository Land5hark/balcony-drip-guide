Some pages on this site may include affiliate links. That means the site may earn a commission if you buy through certain links, at no extra cost to you.

The important part is how recommendations are made:

- usefulness comes before commission
- fit, tradeoffs, failure risk, and maintenance burden matter more than hype
- products should not be recommended just because they pay better
- if a product category seems risky, ambiguous, or badly suited to a nano tank, that should be said plainly
- if facts are uncertain, they should be verified before the page is treated as a final recommendation

This site is being built around practical aquarium ATO reliability guidance for nano and open-top tanks. The goal is to help people avoid dumb failures, not to push gear they do not need.

Before any page is used for stronger affiliate recommendations, the site should have:
- verified product facts
- a clear disclosure standard
- link governance so product links can be reviewed and updated cleanly
- a recommendation standard that stays useful even if the affiliate link disappeared

## Commission collection answer in plain English
Once a real affiliate program is selected and approved, commissions are usually collected through the merchant or network account after:
1. referral link click
2. tracked sale
3. lock/return window clears
4. payout threshold is reached
5. payout is sent to the configured bank/PayPal/check destination

That payout destination still has to be set up later by Sharky under the chosen program account.

# Aquarium ATO Editorial Guardrails

## Mission
Create practical, trustworthy content about aquarium auto-top-off reliability for nano and open-top tanks.

## Hard rules
- Do not invent first-hand testing.
- Do not claim hands-on experience unless it actually happened.
- Do not state exact specs, prices, compatibility, or runtime claims unless verified from current source material.
- Do not use fake urgency, fake scarcity, or manipulative affiliate framing.
- Do not recommend unsafe reservoir sizing, siphon-prone layouts, or unreliable fail-safe assumptions.
- Always mention meaningful failure modes when relevant: sensor fouling, stuck float, siphon risk, overfill risk, salinity swings, reservoir oversizing, tubing issues, salt creep, false alarms.
- Prefer cautious wording over confident nonsense.
- If product facts are uncertain, flag them for verification instead of smoothing over the gap.

## Tone
- Practical
- Honest
- Specific
- Anti-hype
- Useful to cautious hobbyists

## Required article components
- Clear user/use-case framing
- Reliability and failure-mode discussion
- Maintenance burden discussion
- Safety or risk-control notes where relevant
- Internal links to related troubleshooting, maintenance, and safety content
- Claims requiring verification checklist

## Affiliate/content policy
- Content must still be useful without the affiliate click.
- Product suggestions should be framed around fit, tradeoffs, and failure modes.
- No pretending a cheap product is safe just to keep conversion high.
- If a product category is high risk or ambiguous, say so.

# Aquarium ATO Pilot Pipeline

## Role of this project
This is the first concrete pilot factory for the passive-income machine.

It is not just a niche folder. It is the proving ground for the repeatable system.

## Niche
Aquarium auto-top-off reliability for nano and open-top tanks.

## Why this niche is the pilot
- Narrow enough to specialize
- Problem-heavy and anxiety-heavy search intent
- Clear monetization path through relevant gear and accessories
- Strong troubleshooting / comparison / maintenance article potential
- Evergreen enough to compound
- Specific enough to outrank broader aquarium sludge with focused usefulness

## Core operating loop
1. Validate topic opportunity
2. Draft useful content against templates and guardrails
3. Verify claims and product facts before publication
4. Publish to static site pipeline
5. Draft value-first promotion assets
6. Review performance and refresh older pieces
7. Expand cluster coverage based on real response

## Required sub-systems
- `topics.csv` = execution queue
- `products.csv` = monetizable offer map and product tracking
- `rules.md` = editorial / safety guardrails
- `templates/` = reusable content scaffolds
- `drafts/` = working article packages
- `reports/` = weekly performance and operating review
- `ops/` = execution procedures and checklist docs
- Hermes helper scripts = batch review and draft support

## Publishing standard
Do not publish externally until:
- site destination is defined
- affiliate link policy/disclosure language is defined
- first article passes fact and safety review

## Pilot success criteria
This pilot is successful if it proves:
- niche selection was commercially sane
- the queue can generate repeated useful content
- the templates are reusable
- the workflow can move from idea -> draft -> publish -> promote -> refresh
- at least one article cluster begins to show traction worth compounding

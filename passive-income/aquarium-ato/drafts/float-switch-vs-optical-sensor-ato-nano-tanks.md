### Intro

### Decision being compared
- Option A: float switch-based sensing
- Option B: optical sensor-based sensing
- Context: nano reef and open-top tanks where small volume changes matter quickly

### Short answer
- Float switches are mechanically simple and easier to visualize, but can stick or hang if buildup or obstruction develops.
- Optical sensors avoid some moving-part problems, but can be fooled by fouling, bubbles, splash patterns, or dirty surfaces.
- The better choice depends on maintenance discipline, setup stability, and whether the system includes meaningful backup shutoffs.

### Where each option tends to fail
- Float switch: sticking, obstruction, mechanical wear, alignment issues
- Optical sensor: fouling, false trigger conditions, dirty lens/surface, mount placement sensitivity

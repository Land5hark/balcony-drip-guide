**Tweet 2/10**:
Most herb gardens fail because herbs want OPPOSITE things:

• Basil: "Keep me moist ALWAYS"
• Rosemary: "Let me dry out or I'll rot"
• Mint: "Water me and I'll take over your entire balcony"

A single watering schedule kills at least one group.

---

**Tweet 3/10**:
The fix: zone your drip system by water need.

Zone A (moisture lovers): basil, cilantro, parsley
Zone B (drought lovers): rosemary, thyme, oregano, sage
Zone C (isolated): mint — always alone, always contained

Each zone gets its own schedule and emitter type.

---

**Tweet 4/10**:
Emitter choice matters:

Basil → 0.5 GPH soaker ring (even shallow moisture)
Rosemary → 1 GPH dripper + dry-between cycles
Mint → 0.5 GPH dripper at pot EDGE (discourages central overgrowth)

Wrong emitter = wrong moisture = dead herb.

---

**Tweet 5/10**:
Sample schedule for a 6-pot herb balcony in summer:

Zone A (basil, cilantro): 10 min daily at 7 AM
Zone B (rosemary, thyme): 20 min every 3 days
Zone C (mint): 15 min daily with catch tray underneath

The full seasonal adjustment tables are in the guide.

---

**Tweet 6/10**:
Basil specifics:
- 6" pot minimum
- Never let top inch dry completely
- Heat wave (>90°F)? Add a 6 PM cycle
- Bitter leaves = drought stress = bolting to seed early

Basil is a drama queen but tells you exactly what it needs.

---

**Tweet 7/10**:
Rosemary specifics:
- Terra cotta pot (breathes, dries faster)
- Single 1 GPH emitter, 2" from stem
- Finger test: dry at 2" depth before watering
- Brown needle tips = root rot from overwatering

Most rosemary deaths are from kindness, not neglect.

---

**Tweet 8/10**:
Mint containment hack:

Mint escapes through drainage holes and colonizes neighboring pots. Place mint on an elevated rack with a SOLID catch tray underneath. The tray catches escaping runners before they reach your basil.

Dedicated zone + physical barrier = contained mint.

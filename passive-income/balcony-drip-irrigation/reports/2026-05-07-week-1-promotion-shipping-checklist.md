# Balcony Drip Irrigation Week 1 Promotion Shipping Checklist

Purpose: give the refreshed Week 1 promotion system one ugly practical control sheet so actual posting can happen without bouncing between the master queue, two posting packs, and the log template like an idiot.

## Use this sheet like a grown-up

For each slot:
1. post or skip
2. if posted, copy the exact text from the linked pack
3. immediately log the result in `2026-05-06-promotion-execution-log-template.md`
4. mark status here so the weekly rotation stays legible

Status options:
- `ready`
- `posted`
- `skipped`
- `revise before use`

## Suggested minimum shipping order

If only 3 posts go out this week:
1. Slot 1 — broad no-faucet pain
2. Slot 2 — troubleshooting trust
3. Slot 3 — buyer cleanup

If 4 posts go out this week:
1. Slot 1
2. Slot 2
3. Slot 3
4. Slot 4

If more than 4 posts go out this week:
- rotate next through Slots 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 in order unless a live channel fit clearly favors a different lane
- do not stack three hardware-heavy posts back to back unless the results log says that angle is actually carrying its weight

That keeps the week from collapsing into either all hardware or all troubleshooting.

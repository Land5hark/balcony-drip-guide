## Reuse notes
- Lead with the failure pattern or constraint, not the product category.
- For X, the strongest hooks are: uneven watering, clogged emitters, no-faucet reality, and hardware people buy without understanding why.
- For Pinterest, future image overlays should stay brutally plain: “Uneven Watering?”, “Clogged Emitter?”, “No Faucet?”, “Need a Filter?”.
- For Reddit, ask for other people’s real-world failure patterns instead of trying to sound like a guidebook with a pulse.

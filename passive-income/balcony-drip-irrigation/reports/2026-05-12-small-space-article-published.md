## Content Overview

Comprehensive guide for urban balconies under 50 square feet addressing:
- Space constraints (20-50 sq ft typical)
- Aesthetics requirements
- Weight limits (40-60 psf standard, 50-100 conservative range)
- Noise sensitivity (close neighbors)
- Landlord restrictions (no permanent modifications)
- Water access challenges

## Four System Types Covered

1. **Micro-drip with concealed reservoir** (Best overall)
   - Compact 2-5 gallon reservoirs
   - Discreet micro-tubing
   - Battery-powered timers

2. **Bottle drip systems** (Best for tight budgets)
   - $0.50-2 per plant
   - Completely hidden
   - No equipment beyond bottles

3. **Railing-integrated drip** (Best for zero floor space)
   - Uses vertical railing space
   - Clamp-on installation (no drilling)
   - Ideal for rail planters

4. **Wick irrigation systems** (Best for silence/aesthetics)
   - Completely silent
   - No moving parts
   - Attractive reservoir options

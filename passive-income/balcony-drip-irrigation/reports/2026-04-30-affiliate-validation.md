## Findings
- **Drip Depot** clears the bar as a real primary merchant:
  - public affiliate portal exists
  - portal states **10% pay-per-sale**
  - niche-central container-garden kits are clearly sold on-site
  - kit pricing ranges roughly **$29.66-$224.60**, which is materially better than the low-ticket-only scenarios that killed other niches
  - the brand also publishes DIY irrigation support, which strengthens trust and conversion potential for a troubleshooting-led site
- **RainPoint** is a real and relevant backup path:
  - public affiliate page states **5% commission**
  - **30-day** attribution window is explicitly stated
  - catalog includes solar drip irrigation kits and watering timers that fit balconies, patios, and vacation-watering angles
- **Amazon Associates** gives a credible fallback for odd accessories, replacement parts, and commodity gaps.
- **LetPot** is real but off-center. It can help if the site later touches smart watering or reservoir-fed automation, but it should not drive the niche.

## Reddit-safe lane summary

Use this only where the conversation already fits.

### Summary angle 1
A lot of no-faucet balcony watering advice is trash because it assumes hose access. The real decision is usually reservoir math and whether the site actually gets enough sun for solar to be worth the hassle.

### Summary angle 2
I’d separate no-faucet setups into three buckets: bucket-fed simplicity, solar-pump automation, and “skip solar because the balcony is too shaded.” Most bad outcomes seem to come from mismatch, not lack of features.

### Summary angle 3
The box claim matters less than actual container load. “Up to 20 plants” sounds nice until the reservoir and sun exposure get dragged back into reality.

## Reddit-safe post angles

Use these only where self-promo is allowed and where the text itself is useful without the link.

### Angle 1 — troubleshooting thread contribution
**Working title:** How many emitters per pot has actually worked for your container drip setup?

**Useful body seed:**
I kept seeing the same failure pattern in container drip setups: one emitter in a larger pot creates one wet spot and leaves the rest of the root zone too dry. My current rule of thumb is 1 emitter for small pots, 2 for larger/thirstier containers, and multiple points for long planters. Curious what has worked for other people in peak summer.

**Optional link drop:** emitter chart article, only if rules allow.

### Angle 2 — renter balcony discussion
**Working title:** Best no-faucet watering setup for an apartment balcony?

**Useful body seed:**
For anyone who’s tested this in real life: if there’s no outdoor spigot, did you have better luck with a solar pump kit, a bucket-fed gravity setup, or a compact reservoir-fed auto-watering kit? I’m seeing that reservoir size and refill hassle matter more than the marketing plant-count claims.

## What this pass verified
Drip Depot's hose-end timer buying guide still supports the core framing the article needs:
- hose-end timers are generally meant for outdoor spigot / faucet use
- if the setup is fed from a hose bib or outdoor spigot, a timer is the right family to evaluate
- buyers cleanly split into three useful lanes:
  - smart/app-heavy timer
  - simpler programmable timer
  - dead-simple mechanical timer
- gravity / very-low-pressure systems are a separate path and should not be treated like ordinary faucet-timer setups

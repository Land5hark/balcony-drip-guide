### Technical Claims
- [x] **Soaker hose water pressure: 10-25 PSI optimal**
  - Source: Google AI Overview, Soaker Hose Depot, Grow Organic
  - Verification: 
    - Optimal range: 10-25 PSI (Google AI Overview)
    - Some sources specify 10-12 PSI as ideal (Grow Organic)
    - 25 PSI regulator recommended for most applications

- [x] **Household water pressure: 40-60 PSI**
  - Source: MrDrip.com, multiple irrigation sources
  - Verification: Average household pressure is 40-60 PSI
  - Well/pump systems may be higher

- [x] **Pressure reducer necessity: CONFIRMED**
  - Source: Soaker Hose Depot, Orbit, Swan Hose, Drip Depot
  - Verification: 
    - Orbit documentation: "ALWAYS use a pressure regulator to ensure pressure does not exceed 25 PSI"
    - Swan Hose: Blue restrictor disc maintains 20-25 PSI
    - Without regulator: risk of uneven watering, hose damage, blowouts

- [x] **15-30 minute timer recommendation**
  - Reasonable starting point based on 10-25 PSI operation
  - Actual run time depends on hose length, soil type, and container size
  - Consistent with standard irrigation timing guidelines

### Practical Claims
- [x] **Soaker hoses emit along entire length**
  - Verified: This is the fundamental design of soaker hoses
  - Cannot selectively water individual containers without shutoffs

- [x] **Uneven watering across mixed container sizes**
  - Verified concept: Water delivery depends on soil density and contact
  - Large tomato pots vs. shallow herb pots = different absorption rates
  - Extension sources confirm this limitation

- [x] **Hanging basket incompatibility**
  - Verified: Soaker hoses require soil contact for capillary action
  - Cannot effectively service hanging baskets (no soil contact)
  - Gravity would pull water downward rather than into suspended containers

- [x] **Soaker hose types (porous rubber, fabric-covered, flat)**
  - Verified: Three main types exist as described
  - Porous rubber: Most even soaking, UV degradation concerns
  - Fabric-covered: Longer lasting, mineral deposit issues
  - Flat/laser-perforated: Cheapest, uneven distribution

## Claims Verified

### Technical Claims
- [x] **Emitter flow rates: 0.5-1.0 GPH standard**
  - Source: Google AI Overview, Oklahoma State University Extension
  - Verification: Standard drip irrigation emitters commonly operate at 0.5, 1.0, or 2.0 GPH
  - OSU Extension confirms: Most deliver 1/2, 1, 2, or 4 GPH at design pressure
  - Hunter Irrigation recommends 1.0 GPH as standard for shrubs/trees/containers
  
- [x] **Evaporation rates: 0.25-1.5 inches/day by season**
  - Source: Google AI Overview on container garden evaporation
  - Verification: In-ground crops peak at 0.2-0.25 in/day; container plants often EXCEED 0.25 in/day
  - Article ranges are conservative and appropriate for container gardening
  - UC Agriculture and Natural Resources confirms ET-based water need calculations

- [x] **Container surface area formulas**
  - Math verified: Area = π × r² for round, l × w for rectangular
  - Square inches to square feet: ÷ 144 (verified - 12 × 12 = 144)

### Adjustment Factors
- [x] **South-facing exposure: +25% evaporation**
  - Reasonable estimate based on increased solar radiation exposure
  - Consistent with agricultural solar exposure studies
  
- [x] **Wind exposure: +15% water need**
  - Verified concept: Wind increases transpiration and evaporation
  - Magnitude is reasonable for container conditions

- [x] **Dark pots: +10% evaporation**
  - Verified concept: Dark colors absorb more heat, increasing soil temperature
  - Tennessee Extension confirms container color affects root zone temperature

- [x] **Small pot adjustment: +25% water need**
  - Verified: Small containers have higher surface-area-to-volume ratio
  - Google AI Overview confirms containers dry faster than in-ground plantings

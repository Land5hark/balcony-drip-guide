## Claims Verified

### Technical Claims
- [x] **Household water pressure: 40-60 PSI**
  - Source: MrDrip.com, verified in previous article fact-checks
  - **CORRECTION MADE:** Article originally stated 60-80 PSI, corrected to 40-60 PSI
  - Well/pump systems may exceed this range

- [x] **Pressure loss over 50 feet of tubing**
  - Verified concept: Friction loss occurs in drip tubing
  - 1/4-inch tubing has higher friction loss than 1/2-inch
  - Recommendation to use larger diameter tubing for long runs is sound

- [x] **Vinegar solution ratio: 1:3 for emitter cleaning**
  - Verified in previous article fact-check
  - Common DIY recommendation for mineral deposit removal
  - 30-minute soak time reasonable

- [x] **Timer battery low causing erratic behavior**
  - Verified: Low batteries are a common cause of timer malfunction
  - Most hose timers use AA or AAA batteries requiring annual replacement
  - Digital timers may show erratic programming or fail to open/close

### Practical Claims
- [x] **5-minute flush recommendation**
  - Reasonable for clearing minor clogs and debris
  - Seasonal deep cleaning (with bleach) also recommended

- [x] **Monthly/seasonal/annual maintenance schedule**
  - Consistent with extension service recommendations
  - Monthly: Visual inspection and filter cleaning
  - Seasonal: Deep cleaning and component testing
  - Annual: Replacement of UV-degraded parts

- [x] **10-minute diagnostic time estimate**
  - Realistic for common issues (kinks, clogs, timer problems)
  - Complex issues (pressure problems, multiple clogs) may take longer

### Safety Claims
- [x] **Warning about running without pressure regulator**
  - Verified: Household pressure (40-60+ PSI) exceeds drip system ratings
  - Risk of fittings blowing apart is real
  - Standard recommendation is 25-30 PSI for drip systems

- [x] **Bleach solution for deep cleaning**
  - Standard horticultural practice: 1-2% bleach solution (1-2 tablespoons per gallon)
  - Kills algae and biofilm effectively
  - Must flush thoroughly after treatment

# Verification Notes: What Are Common Problems With Drip Irrigation Systems?

**Writer:** Spike

## Claims Verified

### Technical Claims
- [x] **Standard pressure regulator setting: 25-30 PSI**
  - Source: Google AI Overview, Drip Depot, Hills Irrigation, Oklahoma State University Extension
  - Verification: 25 PSI is the most commonly recommended standard setting
  - Drip systems operate optimally at 10-30 PSI (Hills Irrigation)
  - OSU Extension: 20 PSI with operating range of 15-30 PSI

- [x] **Household water pressure: 40-60 PSI**
  - Source: MrDrip.com, multiple irrigation sources (verified in previous article)
  - **CORRECTION MADE:** Article originally stated 60-80 PSI, corrected to 40-60 PSI
  - Well/pump systems may exceed this range

- [x] **Standard filter mesh: 120-150 mesh**
  - Source: Google AI Overview, Agriculture Victoria, Drip Depot, IrrigationKing, Wes-Tech Irrigation
  - Verification:
    - Standard: 120 mesh (130 microns)
    - Fine/low-flow: 150-200 mesh (74-100 microns)
    - Article range (120-150 mesh) is accurate

- [x] **Drip emitter flow rates: 0.5-2 gallons per hour**
  - Source: Verified in previous article fact-check
  - Standard: 0.5, 1.0, or 2.0 GPH (Oklahoma State University Extension)
  - Hunter Irrigation confirms 1.0 GPH as standard for containers

- [x] **Container water needs: 1-2 gallons per day in summer**
  - Verified in "how-much-water-do-balcony-plants-really-need" article
  - Depends on container size, plant type, and weather conditions
  - Article presents as example/calculation basis, not universal claim

### Practical Claims
- [x] **Monthly system flushing recommendation**
  - Reasonable preventive maintenance schedule
  - Consistent with extension service recommendations
  - Seasonal deep cleaning also recommended

- [x] **Annual emitter replacement as preventive maintenance**
  - Reasonable given UV degradation and mineral buildup
  - Manufacturer recommendations support regular replacement

- [x] **Weekly visual inspection schedule**
  - Standard horticultural practice for irrigation systems
  - Appropriate frequency for container setups

- [x] **70% of failures from pressure/filter issues**
  - **MODIFIED:** Changed to "A pressure regulator and proper filter prevent most common failures"
  - Original 70% statistic removed as unsourced
  - Pressure and filtration are widely cited as primary failure causes

### Safety Claims
- [x] **Vinegar solution for emitter cleaning (1:3 ratio)**
  - Common DIY recommendation for mineral deposit removal
  - Dilute acetic acid effective against calcium/magnesium buildup
  - 30-minute soak time reasonable

- [x] **Plumber's tape application: 3-4 wraps**
  - Standard plumbing practice
  - Clockwise application ensures tape doesn't unwrap when tightening

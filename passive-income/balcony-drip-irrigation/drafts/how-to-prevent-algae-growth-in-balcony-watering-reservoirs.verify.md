## Claims Verified

### Product/Technical Claims
- [x] **Hydrogen peroxide dosing: Start with small amount, conservative approach**
  - Source: Google AI Overview, Hyjo Indoor Garden, Oklahoma State University Extension
  - Verification: General recommendation is 1-4 teaspoons per gallon (5-20 mL per 3.8 liters)
  - Article's conservative approach (start small, increase if needed) is plant-safe
  - OSU Extension: ½ cup per 100 gallons for irrigation systems
  - **Note:** Article recommends starting conservatively which is appropriate for balcony reservoirs

- [x] **Bleach dilution: 1-2 drops per gallon for maintenance**
  - Source: Google AI Overview, extension agencies
  - Verification: 5-7 drops per gallon is standard recommendation (1/8 teaspoon)
  - Article's 1-2 drops is slightly more conservative but within safe range
  - 10% solution (1:9 ratio) for cleaning is standard disinfection strength
  - **Note:** Article appropriately emphasizes caution with bleach

- [x] **Algae growth factors: Light, nutrients, warmth, stagnation**
  - Verified: These are the four primary requirements for algae growth
  - Balcony conditions often provide all four
  - Prevention strategies target these factors correctly

- [x] **UV penetration through plastic**
  - Verified: Clear/translucent containers allow UV penetration
  - Opaque containers (black, dark colors) block UV effectively
  - Wrapping clear reservoirs blocks light and prevents algae

### Practical Claims
- [x] **Monthly reservoir rinsing schedule**
  - Reasonable preventive maintenance frequency
  - Weekly visual checks + monthly cleaning is standard practice
  - Seasonal deep cleaning with bleach appropriate

- [x] **Weekly visual check recommendation**
  - Standard maintenance practice for irrigation systems
  - Appropriate for catching algae early

- [x] **Annual tubing replacement suggestion**
  - Reasonable given UV degradation and biofilm buildup
  - Some sources recommend more frequent replacement for clear tubing

- [x] **First-flush diverter effectiveness for rainwater**
  - Verified concept: Diverters capture initial roof runoff (debris, contaminants)
  - Improves water quality for irrigation

### Safety Claims
- [x] **Food-grade vs stabilized hydrogen peroxide**
  - Verified distinction exists
  - Food-grade (unstabilized) preferred for plant applications
  - Stabilizers in drugstore peroxide may not be plant-safe

- [x] **Plant safety of diluted bleach concentrations**
  - Verified: Low concentrations (5-7 drops/gallon) generally plant-safe
  - Higher concentrations risk plant damage
  - Article appropriately emphasizes caution

- [x] **Aquarium algaecide plant safety warnings**
  - Verified: Not all algaecides are plant-safe
  - Label reading essential
  - Hydroponic/aquarium products vary in plant compatibility

## Notes on Concentrations

**Hydrogen Peroxide:**
- Article recommends conservative starting point
- Industry range: 1-4 teaspoons per gallon
- Balcony reservoirs benefit from lower concentrations due to smaller volumes

**Bleach:**
- Article recommends 1-2 drops per gallon (maintenance)
- Industry standard: 5-7 drops per gallon
- Article's conservative approach is appropriate for edible plants
- 10% solution for cleaning matches standard disinfection practices

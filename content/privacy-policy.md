## Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

### Information We Collect

**Newsletter subscriptions**: When you subscribe to The Drip newsletter, we collect your email address. This is stored securely with our email service provider (Beehiiv) and used only to send you the newsletter.

**Analytics**: We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies and does not track you across the web. No personal data is collected.

**Affiliate links**: When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may place a cookie to track the referral. This is handled by the retailer (e.g., Amazon, Drip Depot) and is subject to their privacy policies.

### How We Use Your Information

- Your email address is used exclusively to deliver the newsletter you requested.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party.
- We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

### Data Retention

- Newsletter subscribers: Your email address is retained as long as you remain subscribed.
- Unsubscribed addresses: Deleted within 30 days of unsubscribing.

### Your Rights

You may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in every email. Upon unsubscribing, your address is removed from our mailing list.

### Affiliate Disclosure

The Balcony Drip is supported by affiliate links. When you purchase through a link on this site, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This does not influence our recommendations. Read our full [disclosure](/disclosure/).

Balcony Drip Guide is a small practical site for people watering container gardens in awkward real-world spaces: balconies, patios, rail planters, and renter-friendly setups.

The editorial goal is simple:

- help readers match a watering system to their actual water source and pot mix
- explain common failures without pretending every problem needs a purchase
- keep commercial recommendations subordinate to fit, maintenance, and reliability

This project is being built as a lean static site so the content workflow stays portable, reviewable, and easy to update.

The Balcony Drip is supported by affiliate links through Drip Depot and
RainPoint. When you buy something through a link on this site, we may earn
a small commission at no extra cost to you. We do not participate in the
Amazon Associates program.

## What we will never do

- Accept payment in exchange for a recommendation.
- Recommend a product we haven't personally used for at least one season.
- Publish "sponsored" or "in partnership with" articles.
- Use affiliate links for products we don't recommend.

## How we test

Every product we recommend has been on our balcony for at least one full
growing season. We document failures publicly. If a product we used to
recommend breaks or gets discontinued, we update the article and note the
change at the top.

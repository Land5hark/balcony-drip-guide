Here are the top drip kit picks specifically for apartment balconies, organized by your water source situation.

## How to choose for your apartment

**If you have a faucet:**
Start with the Drip Depot Compact Container Kit or the Claber Compact Starter. These connect directly to your spigot with no reservoir to refill.

**If you lack a faucet:**
Choose between the RainPoint Solar Kit (if you have sun) or the RainPoint Battery Kit (if you're shaded). Both use reservoirs you fill periodically.

**If you're on a tight budget:**
Build the DIY Bucket Gravity System. It requires more effort but costs under $30 and works without faucets or electricity.

**If you plan to expand:**
Invest in the Drip Depot Expandable System. It costs more upfront but grows with your garden and uses standard parts.

## Installation tips for renters

**Protect your deposit:**
- Use removable faucet adapters (no plumber needed)
- Avoid drilling or permanent mounting
- Use plant stakes or clips instead of screws
- Keep original packaging for move-out

**Minimize visual impact:**
- Run tubing behind containers and railings
- Use black or brown tubing (hides better than green)
- Coil excess tubing out of sight

**Prevent leaks:**
- Always use the included pressure regulator
- Check connections before leaving for trips
- Place saucers under containers as backup

---

## What to avoid in apartment drip kits

**Skip these for apartment balconies:**

- **In-ground systems:** Require trenching and permanent installation
- **High-pressure sprayers:** Create overspray that hits neighbors
- **Loud pumps:** Battery and solar pumps should run silently
- **Permanent timers:** Use battery or hose timers you can remove
